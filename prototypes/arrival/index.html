Land at Smoke Lake

↗ Light crosswind

A gap in the granite. Pines beyond it.

Paddle / steer
Up: paddle · Down: brake

A moment on the lake

Thumb pad or WASD / arrows: up paddles, down back-paddles, left and right steer. Let go to coast.

Settle the canoe beside the shore, then tap the action button or press E / Space to step out. The same button unloads nearby gear and puts it down wherever you stand.

Use Take bow (T) and walk to pull the canoe. Hold the action button to tie its line from dry ground. Board canoe (B) climbs back into a floating canoe.

Watch the waterline. Waves reach the low rock; higher ground stays dry. There is no required landing angle or order. P pauses.
