Sawbill → Smoke · After the portage

The lake opens up

The canoe slips back into the water. Smoke Lake is quieter on this side of the trail. Somewhere ahead is a place to spend the night.

Follow the shore

A thin curl of smoke rises at the first campsite. Farther on, granite reaches the water and the fire grate looks empty.

Swipe down beside the canoe to paddle. A left stroke turns you right; a right stroke turns you left.

Find the open site. Watch the shore as you paddle.

Place the tent

Granite slopes toward the water. The mossy hollow is damp. A worn, level patch sits back toward the pines.

Drag the tent onto the ground where it belongs. You can also move it with the buttons.

Where can six people camp without changing the ground?

A place to stay

The tent sits on the worn, level patch. Dad points to the granite ledge: “That’s where we’ll fish.” You hear the canoe scrape softly as the family pulls it above the waterline.

The shore is yours for the night. There’s time to set up the tarp, and maybe cast from those rocks.

One open campsite. A good landing. Room for the family without clearing anything new.
